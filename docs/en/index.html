What’s different

The owner decides which hours book themselves

Most booking tools apply one rule to the whole shop. BookingYou sets it per time window: weekday afternoons confirm instantly, Saturday evenings wait for the owner’s approval.

For example: Tuesday at 3pm is usually easy to fit in, so it confirms instantly. Saturday evening is your busiest slot, so it waits for your approval. Same shop, different rules by hour.

Enforced on the server, not only in the app

Timing, capacity, closed days and approval rules are checked in the database, so a modified client cannot book around them.

FAQ

You might be wondering

Does the customer need the app to book?

Yes, today. Your link opens the App Store or Google Play if the app is missing. Booking from the web without installing is the next thing we are building.

Can I enter a booking for a customer myself?

Yes. Phone and walk-in bookings go into the same calendar as online ones, and they count in your statistics and customer records.

What if a customer needs a different time?

Both sides can reschedule without cancelling, so the original booking stays intact and your numbers stay honest.

Do you take a commission or hold my money?

No. Customers pay your shop directly. We never handle the money, so there is no commission.

I already list on a booking portal. Can I still use this?

Yes. Portals bring new customers; BookingYou runs every booking including your regulars. They work side by side.
