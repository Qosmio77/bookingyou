Web booking · New

Book in your browser — no app needed

Got a shop’s BookingYou link or QR code? Open it in any browser, on your phone or computer, and book right there. No download and no account.

  1. 1
    Open the shop’s linkTap the BookingYou link the shop shares on Instagram, a messaging app or its website, or scan the QR card at the counter. The booking page opens in your browser.
  2. 2
    Choose a serviceEach service shows its duration and price. Pick the one you want.
  1. 3
    Pick a date and timeOnly times that are still free are shown, in the shop’s local time. Closed days are greyed out.
  1. 4
    Enter your name and phoneThat’s all the shop needs. A note is optional. No sign-up.
  2. 5
    Send it and wait for the shopWeb bookings are confirmed by the shop. After you submit, you get your own booking link — save it to check the status or cancel later.

Good to know

  • There is no account, so your booking link is your key. Save it and don’t share it.
  • Once the shop confirms, the status on your booking link changes from “Waiting for the shop” to “Confirmed”.
  • Some shops take bookings in the app only. The page says so straight away and gives you a button to open the app.
  • Want reminders, chat with the shop and all your bookings in one place? Get the app.

FAQ

Questions you may have

Are the core features really free?

Core features such as booking pages, calendars, bookings on behalf of customers, rescheduling, reminders and approval rules are free. Pro is still planned, not yet available, and its pricing has not been set.

Do customers have to download the app?

No. Customers can use your shared link or QR code to submit a booking in their browser. Individual shops may choose to accept in-app bookings only.

Are web bookings confirmed automatically?

Web bookings always require the shop’s confirmation. In-app bookings can use instant confirmation or merchant approval, depending on the time slot.

Can I enter a booking for a customer myself?

Yes. Phone and walk-in bookings go into the same calendar as online ones, and they count in your statistics and customer records.

What if a customer needs a different time?

Both sides can reschedule without cancelling, so the original booking stays intact and your numbers stay honest.

Do you take a commission or hold my money?

No. Customers pay your shop directly. We never handle the money, so there is no commission.

I already list on a booking portal. Can I still use this?

Yes. Portals bring new customers; BookingYou runs every booking including your regulars. They work side by side.
