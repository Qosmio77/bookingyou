01 / Customer bookings

How do customers book?

  1. Choose a trial or training session

    Clients open the link, check service and location details, duration and price, then choose the session they need.

  2. Request a time you can attend

    Choose a date and available slot, then enter a name and phone number. Notes can mention a first visit or general training goals to help the coach prepare.

  3. Wait for confirmation before finalising plans

    Web bookings require the coach’s confirmation. Keep the booking link to check its status. Contact the coach first if the time needs to change.

02 / Merchant management

How do merchants view and handle bookings?

  1. View new sessions in the merchant app

    Open the pending tab in the booking list and check the client, session type, time and notes.

  2. Check venue and timing before confirming

    Compare the request with confirmed sessions in the calendar and allow for travel and rest. Check numbers and the actual venue for group sessions too.

  3. Update status before and after training

    Use the confirmed list to welcome clients. Reschedule by changing the booking time, then mark the session completed so you can refer to it later.

Submit a request → Merchant confirms → Booking confirmed

Web bookings require merchant confirmation. Customers can download the app to manage bookings, chat and receive reminders. Web guests should keep their booking link.

What else should this industry consider?

Can it automatically schedule weekly sessions in one go?

This page describes choosing a time for each booking, not automatically creating a recurring timetable. Coaches can arrange regular clients one session at a time on agreed dates and check each booking.

Will it automatically deduct sessions from a package?

A completed booking status only means the session is finished. This page does not promise package deductions or membership billing. Handle course and payment records through your existing process.

Can clients book a group session directly?

Set capacity and available slots for the service; the exact workflow depends on the merchant’s settings. The coach still needs to confirm the venue, equipment and teaching arrangements.
