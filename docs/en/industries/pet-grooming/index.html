Booking system for pet grooming salons

Every pet has different needs. Check the details before booking grooming.

Bathing and trimming time depends on size, coat and the pet’s condition. Let owners select a service and leave basic notes, then have the groomer check the time and reception arrangements instead of fitting every pet into the same slot.

Pet grooming

Bring order to the everyday challenges you know.

01

“Bath” tells you nothing about size or coat until arrival

State who each service is suitable for. Ask owners to note the pet’s type, size and requested service, then check the details before confirming.

02

Every pet needs a different amount of time

Set different durations for different service scopes. Contact the owner first when assessment is needed rather than estimating from the shortest slot.

03

The dryer drowns out calls, but you do not want to miss bookings

Offer a link and QR code so owners can submit a request themselves. Groomers can review pending requests together during a suitable break.

04

Grooming time gets confused with collection time

Agree drop-off and collection details when confirming. Allow cleaning, preparation and handover time rather than treating a booking’s start time as a collection promise.

01 / Customer bookings

How do customers book?

  1. The owner chooses a suitable service

    Open the shop’s link and check suitability, details, duration and price before choosing bathing, trimming or a consultation.

  2. Enter the owner’s contact details and pet notes

    Choose a slot and enter a name and phone number. Notes can include the pet’s name, type, size and service needs to help the shop follow up.

  3. Wait for the shop to confirm the arrangements

    Keep the personal booking link to check its status. Follow the shop’s confirmed instructions for drop-off, collection and anything to prepare.

02 / Merchant management

How do merchants view and handle bookings?

  1. Open the booking list to review new requests

    In the pending tab, check the owner’s contact details, service, time and notes to understand the preparation needed.

  2. Check the pet’s needs and the day’s capacity

    Contact the owner if anything is unclear. Confirm service time, capacity and handover arrangements before approving the booking.

  3. Work from the day’s confirmed records

    Check today’s appointments in the calendar or confirmed list. Reschedule changes and mark the booking completed after the service.

Submit a request → Merchant confirms → Booking confirmed

Web bookings require merchant confirmation. Customers can download the app to manage bookings, chat and receive reminders. Web guests should keep their booking link.

What else should this industry consider?

Does the system estimate grooming time by breed automatically?

In this workflow, merchants set service durations. Time is not estimated automatically from breed, coat or matting. Speak with the owner before confirming whenever you need more detail.

How many pets can be included in one booking?

Do not treat a general party-size field as a pet count. For several pets, contact the shop first so the merchant can confirm services, time slots and whether separate bookings are needed.

Is this a veterinary appointment system?

This page describes pet bathing and grooming schedules. It does not provide diagnosis, medical records or medical triage. Contact a qualified veterinarian directly about pet health concerns.
