01 / Customer bookings

How do customers book?

  1. Read the service details first

    Customers open your link, review haircut, colour consultation or treatment durations and prices, then choose the service they need.

  2. Choose a date and enter booking details

    Choose an available slot and enter a name, phone number and optional note. For example, mention a colour consultation so you can understand the request before confirming.

  3. Submit and wait for your confirmation

    A web request is not confirmed immediately. Customers keep their personal booking link to check its status or cancel using the options on that page.

02 / Merchant management

How do merchants view and handle bookings?

  1. Open “Bookings” in the merchant app

    Review new requests in the pending list, checking the service, date, time, customer details and notes without matching everything back to private messages.

  2. Confirm against your actual capacity

    Check the day’s confirmed appointments, then assess staff, seats and treatment preparation. Contact the customer first if anything is unclear.

  3. Keep the day’s schedule up to date

    View confirmed appointments in the booking list or calendar. Reschedule when a customer needs a different time and mark the booking completed after the service.

Submit a request → Merchant confirms → Booking confirmed

Web bookings require merchant confirmation. Customers can download the app to manage bookings, chat and receive reminders. Web guests should keep their booking link.

What else should this industry consider?

Can it automatically assign a specific stylist or treatment room?

This workflow centres on services, time slots and merchant confirmation. The shop must confirm staff, seats and rooms; an available slot does not mean these resources have been assigned automatically.

How can customers book when colour duration and price are not yet known?

Create a consultation service first, or explain pricing conditions in the service description. Check the customer’s needs before confirming the full service instead of using one duration for every colour appointment.

Do customers have to download the app?

When the merchant enables web booking, customers can submit a request in a browser without downloading the app or registering. Merchants review and confirm bookings in the BookingYou app.
