Every industry has its own booking rhythm.

From beauty treatments to lessons, choose your industry to see how customers request bookings, how merchants confirm them, and how to handle everyday scheduling challenges.

Beauty & hair salons

One customer needs two hours for colour; another only wants a haircut. Beauty treatments have different preparation needs too. BookingYou brings services, durations and bookings together. Customers request a suitable time, and you confirm it around the day’s schedule.

See the full process →

Nail studios

A customer books plain colour, then arrives needing removal and an intricate design. It is a familiar challenge for nail studios. Make the scope clear when customers choose a service, and review notes before confirming so the schedule reflects the work involved.

See the full process →

Personal trainers

Trials, regular training and small-group sessions need different capacities and arrangements. Working across locations also takes travel time. BookingYou lets clients submit their own requests, then you check the day’s timetable and confirm, leaving more room to discuss their training.

See the full process →

Tutoring & teaching

Parents book trials for their children, students change make-up lesson times, and teachers need room to prepare. BookingYou brings each lesson request and its status together, so you can check the subject, level and time before confirming.

See the full process →

Pet grooming

Bathing and trimming time depends on size, coat and the pet’s condition. Let owners select a service and leave basic notes, then have the groomer check the time and reception arrangements instead of fitting every pet into the same slot.
