01 / Customer bookings

How do customers book?

  1. Parents or students choose a lesson

    Open the teacher’s or studio’s link, choose a service by subject, level or trial type, then view available dates.

  2. Enter the contact person’s details

    Enter a name and contact number. Parents booking for a child can add the student’s name, year level and general learning needs in the notes, without unrelated personal information.

  3. Keep the link to check confirmation

    Wait for the teacher to confirm after submitting. Keep the booking link and coordinate with the teacher first if a make-up lesson or time change is needed.

02 / Merchant management

How do merchants view and handle bookings?

  1. Review pending lessons and notes

    The merchant booking list shows the service, time, contact person and notes. First distinguish trials, regular lessons and other session types.

  2. Check level and teaching arrangements

    Check the subject, student’s level, class capacity and classroom availability. Contact the parent for any missing details before confirming the request.

  3. Follow lessons and make-ups in the calendar

    Review confirmed lessons each day. Check the new date after rescheduling, then mark taught lessons completed for later reference.

Submit a request → Merchant confirms → Booking confirmed

Web bookings require merchant confirmation. Customers can download the app to manage bookings, chat and receive reminders. Web guests should keep their booking link.

What else should this industry consider?

Can parents book for their children?

A parent can submit a web request as the contact person and add the student’s name or year level in the notes. The teacher checks the actual lesson arrangements when confirming.

Can this replace a full student management system?

This page covers lesson bookings and scheduling, not grades, homework, attendance reporting or tuition fee management. Handle those records separately through your teaching process.

Will a full term’s timetable be generated automatically?

This workflow is based on individual bookings. Create and confirm regular lessons on agreed dates one at a time; one request does not automatically schedule the entire term.
